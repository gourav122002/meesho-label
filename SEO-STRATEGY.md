# SEO Strategy

## Competitor observations

Quick Label Crop has a useful SEO structure: category/tool navigation, a specific Meesho tool page, an invoice-specific variant, blog content, and legal/contact pages. Its homepage also creates internal links to the core tool pages. The crawled pages are heavily repetitive and some contain little unique explanatory content, so the opportunity is to keep the useful architecture while raising content quality and technical SEO.

## Recommended positioning

Primary positioning:
**Free Meesho label cropper that creates 4 shipping labels on one A4 landscape page.**

Supporting positioning:
**Invoice-aware Meesho PDF cropper that processes files in the browser.**

## Technical SEO

- SSR/SSG through Next.js.
- Unique metadata per route.
- Canonical URLs.
- Sitemap and robots.
- Crawlable anchor navigation.
- Structured data without fake review data.
- Clean URL hierarchy.
- Mobile-first CSS.

Google documents that JavaScript sites can be crawled and rendered, but also notes that server-side or pre-rendering is a strong approach because it improves what crawlers and users receive in the initial response. It also recommends crawlable links, sitemaps, descriptive titles/meta descriptions, semantic HTML and structured data where relevant.

## Content strategy

Publish useful articles that answer seller questions instead of generating dozens of near-duplicate pages. Update those articles when the Meesho PDF layout changes.

## Internal linking

Home -> Meesho tool -> A4 tool -> invoice tool -> guides.

Every guide should link back to the relevant tool with descriptive anchor text.

## Search Console plan

- verify domain
- submit sitemap
- monitor indexing
- monitor clicks/impressions/CTR/queries
- inspect new pages
- fix crawl/indexing errors

## Do not do

- keyword stuffing
- fake reviews
- doorway pages
- copied competitor text
- hidden text
- dozens of thin marketplace/location pages
- unsupported claims about Meesho affiliation
